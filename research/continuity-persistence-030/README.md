# Continuity 0.3.0 persistence research

## Which randomness must survive?

Start with the responder's rekey encapsulation. Once the response is selected, its exact signed wire, authenticated offer binding and pending root suffice for the existing ready phase. The preceding encapsulation and signature coins have no further role in that phase. Moving their generation into volatile memory requires changing what a peer can accept and what an already acknowledged reservation means. This is a research candidate for a versioned control flight.

The reference contract and source map are pinned at `f70fa0970d7cbe27718309b8da11e7db4288c0e7`. The distributed native baseline uses the `abi2-platforms-v0.2.0` release at tag commit `7c154548d03354a03e91f086b000197026884c0f`. Earlier disclosure and timing experiments have their own source identities. **Recovery information bound.** Fix the authenticated inputs that will still be available after a crash, including any accessible external storage. If two admissible histories leave the same recovery view and require different exact outputs or different answers to a future admissible input, a recovery algorithm on that view cannot always return the correct answer for both. Fixing the algorithm's random tape gives the same contradiction for zero-error randomized recovery. With `k` distinguishable required continuations, a fixed-width remainder must have at least `ceil(log2(k))` bits. This bounds information across the whole recovery boundary. An authenticated digest can identify bytes held elsewhere. Recovering those bytes requires that an authorized reader can actually retrieve them.

A smaller first experiment would replace only the 245-byte phase-4 encapsulation reservation with the original slot and authenticated offer. Retain the phase-5 body, pending root and exact signing reservation, then invoke the signer on that one selected body. An independent signer that enforces this gate could offer a cheaper way to authorize a selected response. The current API passes `&DeviceSigningKey` into the entire computation, so a full process-memory disclosure requires a separate signer boundary. Even in the gated design a readable signature between signing and the final ready write calls for a proof that phase 5 already provides exact recoverability, or a protected mechanism that durably records the signature before exposing it. This variant and the versioned receipt variant have separate proof obligations. Replacing the phase-4 record alone need not reduce the number of durable writes. ## A peer-verifiable decision

**Acceptance information bound.** Consider two histories of the same original slot. In one the computed output is durably selected. In the other the process loses that attempt before selection. Suppose a pre-commit RAM observation supplies all bytes needed by the verifier, and its other trusted inputs and local state are identical in the two histories. Give the verifier the same input and random tape. Its decision is identical. It therefore cannot both accept the selected case and exclude the lost case. For an otherwise acceptable speculative output, some verifier-visible fact must depend causally on selection, or the preceding durable state must already fix the unique admissible result under the stated contract. This is a causal bound. A protected signer, a batch receipt or a query to a durable peer could each supply that fact under different trust and availability assumptions. It gives no universal per-message network round-trip count.

1. Reserve one logical slot by original installation, session, direction, operation and step. Bind the exact inputs, their provenance and the predecessor. Retry under that slot across writer-fence and authorization changes. Using a new attempt, fence or policy version as a new uniqueness namespace permits two decisions for one original operation.
2. An authorized owner computes a proposal from independent attempt randomness. The proposal may be fully visible in RAM. Stage its exact wire and necessary private continuation in a recoverable record. Any durable staging area, WAL, checkpoint or backup belongs to the acquisition interface starting at its first write.
3. A decision authority independent of the disclosed process performs a durable, non-equivocating compare-and-set on the slot. Bind the profile/version, original input commitment, purpose/role, exact wire commitment, predecessor, authorization and fence, and an authenticated, retrievable continuation reference. A private-state commitment must hide guessable inputs/metadata where required. The authority needs a justified basis for certifying durable preparation. A client assertion and a root digest alone do not establish availability.
4. The peer checks a domain-separated receipt for this exact slot and output, alongside ordinary protocol authentication and the live authorization required for this action. The acceptance or effect transaction must have a defined ordering relative to revocation. An offline historical signature cannot establish instantaneous knowledge of a subsequent revocation. Legacy verifiers that accept the bare bytes must be excluded through authenticated negotiation and a distinct signing domain.
5. Release and recover the exact selected envelope. If a certificate is part of the exact public output, either persist its **exact bytes** before they can be accepted, or provide a uniquely reproducible encoding and certificate from an independent durable decision. Two randomized signatures on the same certified statement can have different wire bytes. Define the attested inner wire separately from the outer receipt to avoid a self-referential hash.
6. On unknown outcome, reconcile the original slot with its prepared record. Recompute with new coins only after a negative resolution that also invalidates every still-admissible prior proposal. A read showing an empty slot leaves an in-flight earlier request eligible. If the decision authority is unavailable, the operation waits. A lost acknowledgement can recover the decided value without granting new authority to dispatch it.

For the protected decision abstraction, two accepted outputs for the same slot must match durable decisions on that slot. The compare-and-set admits at most one value. Binding of the receipt and wire therefore makes the two accepted wires identical, up to the assumed forgery/collision bounds in a concrete instantiation. Durable preparation supplies its exact continuation after local process loss. Invalidating older proposals at negative resolution orders retries against any delayed request. These are conditional arguments. Implementing the decision independently, persisting the certificate, validating the compound wire and relating real acquisitions to the abstract observations are open obligations.

An observer can still see two **generated** candidate wires or learn the selected pending root from RAM before the decision. A receipt can exclude the losing wire from peer acceptance. It cannot undo computation, disclosure, nonce reuse or the existence of a valid inner signature. If the original theorem quantifies over every generated signature, the projection to accepted outputs is too weak. If the compromised process can authorize arbitrary new inputs at the decision service, uniqueness alone provides no honest intent guarantee. A rollbackable local-only deployment needs an independently justified authority to offer this stronger profile. In 0.2.0 the signed Witness head names the encrypted image, fence and revision. It does not directly attest a peer-readable response and its availability.

## Independent persistence work

For incremental storage, define the abstraction by replaying authenticated, predecessor-linked committed deltas from the selected checkpoint. Retain a separate projection of prepared work, per-session secret origins, readable stale copies and public commitments. A pending target can already disclose a secret while contributing zero committed state transitions. Model partial writes, repeated crashes during recovery, old snapshots and competing owners under each original protection profile.

For a batch, exhibit a serial order whose every prefix passes the same authorization, one-time-use, budget, current-fence and control-progress predicates. A shared sync may establish durability for multiple independent operations. Combining logical Witness advances requires explicit support for the whole ordered transition and a way to recover every selected member. Preserve every required intermediate external observation and release each member only after its barriers. Report RPC count, durable Witness mutations and framed bytes separately.

Checkpoint replacement must make the new root and its referenced records durable before switching the selected manifest. Retire old records only when every pending operation, exact reply, unsettled epoch, authority lineage and application deduplication obligation remains recoverable. Keep the relevant monotonic floors. Physical pages, backups, abandoned staging and swap retain their acquisition semantics. A crash during compaction or its recovery must satisfy the same relation again.

The proposed theorem has two parts. A forward simulation maps committed operations, externally visible results and faults into legal reference traces. A separate online simulator must account for joint secret acquisition, ciphertexts, public commitments, origin/copy history and correlations across adaptive observations. A one-sided safety trace inclusion alone cannot transfer computational secrecy or establish liveness. Progress needs a fair bounded scheduler, available durable storage and the required fresh authorized interactions. Recovery refinement has precedent in storage systems. The added research question is which cryptographic observations and acquisition boundaries that method must preserve.
