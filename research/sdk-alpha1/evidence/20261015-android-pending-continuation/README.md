# Continued traffic after pending recovery

Two actual-package routes passed the composed lifecycle: signed TCP/fixed SDK
trust/unapplied original write, and TLS/recoverable SDK trust/already-applied
original write. They executed 121 Java/JNI calls, including four abrupt process
exits. The accepted API 37 arm64 phone passed the TLS route in 60 distinct ART
processes, including two abrupt exits. One current phone suffices for this
physical acceptance; no API 36 or second-phone gap is assigned.

Each fresh cohort creates its own identity and registration, establishes actual
traffic to the installed C receiver and preserves one delivery whose application
effect committed but whose receipt was lost. The R2 peer-roster update becomes
pending. After real P0 expiry, exact recovery survives a held-Query process exit
and a lost confirmation result. Expired activation remains refused.

Recovery is followed by the existing independent protocol-policy renewal. Both
account and policy roots approve the original request; the witness's lost commit
reply is reconciled under the original operation. Damaged approval, substituted
scope and cancellation still fail without changing that request. Activation
requires the selected current policy and account authority. The native receiver
adopts the corresponding policy before P0 expiry through the existing receiver
renewal and actual configuration-client path. P0 is not extended.

The same Android identity and session then confirm the original message. Its
business effect remains byte-identical, with no second application of that
message. The two endpoints perform epoch 1 rekey, close/reopen their owners and
exchange a fresh message ID carrying `continued after pending recovery`. The C
receiver reports a newly created effect. Exactly two effects remain: the original
operation and the new post-rekey operation. This proves the specified functional
continuation; a completed rekey is not a PCS proof.

The public readback binds original registration, journal, session and message,
the proposal's witness identity/key commitment and subject, independent approvals,
actual expiry/adoption observations and both peer receipts. It checks all 37
continuation calls in addition to the 24 or 23 original recovery calls. Rekey and
fresh-traffic receipts are required; old-message recovery alone cannot pass.
