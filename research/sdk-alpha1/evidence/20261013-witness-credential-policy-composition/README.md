# Witnessed policy renewal after completed credential renewal

A required-witness installation can now complete a plain credential renewal G and then an independently authorized policy renewal P without changing its original journal, owner or prekey identity. Previously three layers either rejected all retained G state or used the original credential C0 as the current credential. The implementation now retains C0 for immutable storage/witness identity and authenticates C1 for current admission, exact G completion, current roster membership and both P approvals. Pending G, joint continuation and unauthorized substitutions remain refused. Policy terminal state is persisted before acknowledgement; Applied and Closed remain distinct during reply loss and recovery.

Native coverage includes preparation, commit, activation, processed/unprocessed Commit and ACK loss, cancellation, another exact policy request, stale pre-G approvals, stale witnesses and local revocation. Eight actual child-process SIGKILL cuts cover Applied/Closed at observed, terminal, retired and complete cross-store boundaries; the parent reopens the original state after expiry. A bilateral session case performs G then P on both endpoints while retaining an original message and PQ control offer, replays their original bytes, reaches epoch 1 and exchanges messages in both directions. This is session continuity, not a claim of post-compromise secrecy.

The composition has explicit persistent formats: QPANC018 for witness images with credential history plus independent policy state, and QPRHST08 for P retaining a preceding G. QPRHST06 retains its existing meaning of acknowledged P followed by G. Decoders refuse authenticated histories relabelled with the wrong operation order or an older witness version. Only QPANC018 permits an empty closure section; the older QPANC017 restriction is unchanged. This does not establish deployed old/new binary or device migration compatibility.

The policy request path reuses the original enrollment/journal request preparation rather than duplicating current-credential assembly. Adopted P retains its exact signed G so normal traffic checks do not scan every device's renewal history and remain valid after active history retirement. No measured performance claim is made.
