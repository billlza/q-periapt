# Adaptive target accounting: conditional reduction and source correspondence

The [manual argument](../../../../docs/continuity/ADAPTIVE_TARGET_ACCOUNTING_V1.md)
connects fixed-pair component bounds to an adaptively selected classical target,
under explicit finite invocation budgets and efficiently evaluable source/event
classifiers. It does not turn the fixed-pair virtual game into a native protocol
simulation or complete the computational secrecy theorem.

The winning-event bound partitions the single real experiment by the original
generation/encapsulation pair. Its random-component terms remain probabilities
in their own override games. An additional centered-score bound handles an
application-bit guessing experiment without summing the baseline one half as
though it were a negligible winning probability. Unattributed-origin error and
the fixed-pair correctness terms remain explicit.

Neither bound conditions away failures or assumes that matching-target
probabilities from different guessed worlds form one normalized distribution.
The selector, eligibility and origin tests are recomputed in each experiment.
They must be efficiently evaluable without unknown KEM coins or the primitive
challenger's hidden bit. An unresolved acquisition cannot be converted into a
product error or an automatic zero result.
