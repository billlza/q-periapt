# Journal of Cryptology layout decision

Checked on 7 November 2026 against the journal's current submission guidelines, Springer Nature's author support page, and a recent published article.

## Adopted manuscript layout

Retain the single-column Springer Nature authoring layout provided by the unchanged `sn-jnl.cls`. The manuscript uses the journal's numerical citations and a bibliography ordered alphabetically by author surname, with full author lists. The title and reading-copy filenames remain stable throughout research development.

The journal-specific submission page strongly encourages an English PDF prepared with LaTeX or a similar system. It specifies a 150–250-word abstract, 5–10 keywords, author names and affiliations, and the corresponding author's email. Source files are required at acceptance. Its current instructions leave the column count and exact class selection to the submitted manuscript's preparation.

Springer Nature recommends its general LaTeX authoring template. This establishes the provenance of the selected class; journal-specific editorial requirements remain those on the JoC page.

## Published-article comparison

The publisher PDF of Yaobin Shen, Lei Wang and Dawu Gu, *Security Analysis of NIST Key Derivation Using Pseudorandom Functions*, Journal of Cryptology 39, article 31 (2026), uses a single-column text layout. The publisher's 48-page PDF was downloaded and its second page was rendered and inspected. Its final typesetting includes journal headers and publication metadata; the working manuscript uses the authoring template's corresponding title-page and body conventions.

This comparison supports retaining a single-column manuscript. The accepted article's final production typography remains the publisher's typesetting stage.
