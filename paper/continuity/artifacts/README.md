# Evidence in this directory

The directories contain proof notes, source snapshots, executable checks,
verifier outputs and measured data. Each result must be read with its stated
inputs, source revision and observation interface.

The review notes produced during manuscript preparation are internal automated
checks. Historical names such as `independent_review`, `author_check` and
`author_identity` do not establish independent human peer review, verification
by Ziang Li, or approval to submit. Statements and numerical results must be
assessed through their arguments, original measurements and executable evidence.

Historical hash inventories identify the versions on which those checks ran.
They do not certify later editorial changes. The current manuscript's build
and source export have their own file identities; the original experiment
inputs and outputs retain their recorded identities.
