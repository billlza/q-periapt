\subsection*{Data and code availability}
The \href{https://github.com/billlza/q-periapt/tree/947297e59be9d7fc8febb5d81a4e2b945e328208/paper/continuity}{fixed evidence distribution}
contains the proof notes, source maps, finite checks and execution
packages used in this manuscript, including the latency and bootstrap
supplements. Appendix~\ref{app:artifacts} gives its complete commit identity
and reproduction entry point. For each experiment, the archive identifies
the source revision and includes the execution receipt. The accompanying manuscript source
archive binds its files through \path{SHA256SUMS.json}; the manuscript CI
artifact records the checked revision in \path{source-commit.txt}.

\subsection*{AI-assisted preparation}
OpenAI ChatGPT was used for language editing, exploratory proof development,
source-code inspection and \LaTeX{} preparation. The explicit proofs,
mechanized certificates and executable checks are identified separately
so that their respective claims and verification records can be inspected.
